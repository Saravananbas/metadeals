## 3. What you can complain about

- A deal that is wrong, expired, misleading, or a link that is broken;
- A comment or a submitted deal that is unlawful, abusive, infringing, or fake;
- Our handling of your personal data, including a request to see, correct or delete it, or to withdraw consent;
- Anything on the Service that you think breaks the law or our own rules.
