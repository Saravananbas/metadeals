## 1. Why these rules exist

People use MetaDeals4U to decide where to buy. Deals and comments from our community help, but only if they are honest and current. These rules explain what you can share, how we review it, and what happens next.

## 2. Who can share

You need a MetaDeals4U account, a verified email address and to be **18 or older** [OWNER TO CONFIRM and COUNSEL]. You share on your own responsibility and agree to the [Terms of Use](03-terms-of-use.md).

## 3. Sharing a deal

1. Open **Your profile → Share a deal** and fill in the details: title, link to the store's product page (https), price, MRP (if you know it), store name, category, coupon (if any), a description and **one image**.
2. Share only **real, current deals that you have checked on the store's page**. The link must go to the product, not to a search page or a short link that hides the destination.
3. **Use an image you have the right to use.** Do not copy another site's photos or watermarked images without permission. If you are not sure, take your own picture or ask us.
4. **Do not include personal details** (yours or anyone else's) in the text or image.
5. **No pay-to-post, no spam.** Do not post the same link many times, or deals from a store that you are paid by without telling us (see §5).

## 4. What we do with your submission

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Sent for review** | Your deal is saved privately. **It is not public.** You see it under *My submissions* as **Awaiting review**. |
| **Checked** | A member of our team checks the link, price, product and image against the store, and checks these rules. We aim to reply within **[DAYS] [OWNER TO CONFIRM]**. |
| **Approved** | We prepare a draft deal (we may correct the title, price, category or description). It goes live only after a second check and publish step. You see **Approved, going live soon**, then **Live**. We may remove it later if it expires or turns out to be wrong. |
| **Not approved** | We tell you **why**, in plain words. You can **edit and resubmit** from *My submissions*. |

**Approval is our editorial decision. We do not promise to publish, to publish quickly, or to keep a deal live.** We may take a live deal down, for example when the price changes, the offer ends, the store objects, or we receive a valid complaint.

## 5. Things you must tell us

If you have a connection with the store or the product (you work for it, are paid by it, are related to the owner, or earn from the link), say so in the description. We may label such a deal.

## 6. What is not allowed

- False, expired or misleading deals; fake discounts; fake coupon codes; invented scarcity or popularity.
- Anything unlawful, defamatory, hateful, obscene, or that threatens or harasses someone.
- Anything that infringes another person's copyright, trade mark or privacy, or that impersonates a person or a brand.
- Links to adult content, gambling, weapons, counterfeit goods or other products that are banned or restricted in India, or to phishing or malware.
- Personal data of other people.
- Repeated spam, or trying to trick the review (for example showing a good page to us and a different page to shoppers).

## 7. Comments [ONLY IF ENABLED]

Be respectful and keep to the deal. Do not post personal details, links to other sites for promotion, or abuse. We may remove comments that break these rules. You can report a comment with the **Report** link under it.

## 8. Reporting a deal or comment, and corrections

- Use **Report this deal / Report this comment** on the page, or write to **[EMAIL]**. Say what is wrong and, if you can, what the right information is.
- We acknowledge within **24 hours** and aim to resolve within **15 days**. For serious matters (for example content that exposes private images or impersonates someone) we aim to act sooner. [OWNER TO CONFIRM and COUNSEL: align with IT Rules timelines.]
- **Corrections.** If a published deal is wrong we correct it or take it down and, where we can, tell the person who submitted it.
- **Copyright or other rights.** If you are a rights owner, send: your name and contact details; what is yours and where you hold the right; the exact page; why you say it infringes; and a statement that you are the owner or acting for the owner. We will review quickly. We may tell the person who posted the content and let them respond [COUNSEL: add counter-notice steps].
- **If we decline your report or remove your content,** you can ask for a review through the Grievance Officer: [Contact and Grievance](06-contact-and-grievance.md).

## 9. Consequences

For a first minor breach we may just decline or remove the content. For repeated or serious breaches we may limit sharing, suspend or close the account, and where the law requires or permits, tell the authorities. We will tell you the reason unless the law prevents it.

## 10. Your content and your data

See the Terms of Use §7 (licence to display) and the Privacy Policy (what we keep about submissions and for how long). You can ask us to remove your submission, subject to what we must keep by law.
